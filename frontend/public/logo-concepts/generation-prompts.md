# ChainCode AI logo exploration prompts

Mode: built-in ImageGen, used for raster concept exploration only. The selected concept will be rebuilt manually as SVG geometry.

## Shared brief

- Brand: ChainCode, a coding-practice platform where accepted solutions can mint as NFTs.
- Output: exactly eight isolated black logo silhouettes in a strict 4-by-2 grid on pure white.
- Standard: one memorable idea, distinct silhouette, balanced negative space, viable at 16px and 32px.
- Avoid: gradients, shadows, glow, 3D, mockups, watermarks, literal chains, infinity signs, code brackets, coins, shields, badges, cubes, generic typed letters, duplicated constructions, and Arc logo imitation.

## Concepts 01–08: folded ribbon

Create eight original abstract CC monograms built from one continuous folded ribbon. The two Cs should be discovered through overlap or negative space, not placed side by side. Borrow only the high-level lesson from Arc Browser: a recognizable letter emerging from a few broad curved bands with one purposeful crossing.

## Concepts 09–16: asymmetric CC

Create eight abstract folded-band monograms using two or three broad rounded strokes with a single deliberate over-under crossing. The mark should imply CC through rhythm and countershape, with energetic asymmetry and no resemblance to an existing logo.

## Concepts 17–24: shared pivot

Create eight marks derived from one shared pivot. Three broad curved arms sweep toward the right so the top and middle arms imply one C, while the middle and bottom arms imply a second C. Vary tension, proportion, overlap, and negative space without producing an ordinary E glyph.

## Concepts 25–32: negative space

Create eight CC monograms from a single solid geometric mass with clever negative-space cuts. Suggest two Cs without drawing two ordinary letters. Use distinct outer silhouettes, one or two internal cuts at most, and thick forms that survive 16px.

## Corrective edit for concepts 25–32

Change only the generated canvas background from black to pure white while preserving all eight black silhouettes, geometry, positions, spacing, and edges.
