## Notes

- The single clearest pattern: **retention comes from streaks + leaderboards; legitimacy comes
  from verifiable credentials; depth comes from curation + gated solutions.** No platform in
  the catalog combines all three with on-chain certificates — that intersection is ChainCode's
  lane.
- Second clearest: certificates that aren't independently verifiable (plain PDFs) are being
  displaced by W3C VC / OB3 standards. Our ERC-721 already beats PDFs technically — but only
  once metadata, art, and a verify page make it *checkable* by a non-crypto recruiter.
- The wargame category (Ethernaut) is the strongest precedent for our core loop: **solve →
  mint trophy NFT** has run for years with real pull — but Ethernaut's trophies carry no
  metadata, no verify page, and no recruiter surface. That gap is exactly what P0 above fills.
- Pricing anchors observed (Aug 2026): NeetCode Pro $119/yr, LeetCode Premium $179/yr,
  AlgoExpert ~$99/yr, Mimo ~$9.99/mo, POK $0.80–1.50 per NFT credential. Free-with-verifiable-
  upsell is the dominant shape.
