Can two outputs be 100% unique? And when do they collide?

Uniqueness is arithmetic, not magic

Exact duplicates are only impossible if you enforce a dedupe registry. Otherwise the birthday paradox rules: collision chance ≈ n² / 2M, where n = tokens minted and M = size of the output space.

Where collisions actually come from

  • Small discrete spaces. Combos under ~n² will collide by birthday bound. Low-res grids and thin trait sets are the usual culprits.
  • Seed reuse. Seeding by username/email means renames or name-collisions silently regenerate different art. Seed by immutable tokenId + salt instead.
  • Low-entropy PRNG. mulberry32 holds a 32-bit state = max ~4 billion distinct streams. Fine for 10k, thin for public mint-your-own. Use 128-bit+ seeds (hash of tokenId + salt).
  • Perceptual near-dupes. Continuous params cluster visually: same palette + similar superformula exponents read as siblings even when pixel-exact uniqueness holds. Fix: perceptual-hash (pHash) every render at mint time, reject anything below a Hamming-distance threshold from existing supply.
  • Kill list: dedupe registry + pHash distance check + 128-bit seeds + reserved legendary combos = collisions structurally dead.
